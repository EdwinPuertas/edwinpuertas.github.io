## Overview

MentalGuard proposes a multimodal artificial intelligence approach for the early detection of mental health risks in university students. The project overcomes the limitations of traditional assessment tools, which rely on personal interactions with mental health professionals and can be affected by perceived stigma and subjectivity.

## Problem

Universities face increasing rates of depression, anxiety, and related disorders among students. Traditional screening relies on self-report questionnaires and clinical interviews — methods that are slow, costly, and subject to social desirability bias.

## Approach

MentalGuard integrates advanced natural language processing techniques to analyze:

- **Text channels**: Linguistic patterns, lexical features, and semantic markers associated with depression, anxiety, and stress
- **Audio channels**: Prosodic and acoustic features (pitch, speech rate, voice quality) correlated with affective states

Both channels are fused into a unified risk indicator that is more objective and reproducible than subjective clinical instruments.

## Key Features

- **Multimodal fusion**: Combines text and speech signals for robust risk prediction
- **Transformer-based NLP**: Fine-tuned models for Spanish-language clinical and informal text
- **Scalability**: Designed to process large student cohorts with minimal human intervention
- **Privacy-preserving**: Processes signals locally; no raw audio or text leaves the institution

## Expected Outcomes

- Earlier identification of at-risk students before symptoms escalate
- Decision support for counselors and mental health teams
- Foundations for personalized intervention and rehabilitation programs
- Assistive technologies adapted to individual cognitive profiles

## Impact

MentalGuard addresses mental health in academic settings through emerging technologies and cognitive approaches, with the potential to reduce dropout rates and improve overall student well-being.

An energy production company expanded its technological horizons by developing an innovative solution to improve traffic enforcement in urban pedestrian areas. The project focused on addressing the challenge of unauthorized vehicle entries, including motorcycles and other vehicles, which compromise pedestrian safety.

**Start:** January 2023 — **Status:** Active

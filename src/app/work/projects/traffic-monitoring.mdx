## Overview

This project developed an innovative solution to improve traffic enforcement in urban pedestrian areas. The system automatically detects unauthorized vehicle entries — including motorcycles and other vehicles — that compromise pedestrian safety in designated zones of Cartagena de Indias, Colombia.

## Problem

Pedestrian areas in Colombian cities face persistent unauthorized vehicle incursions, particularly by motorcycles. Manual enforcement is costly, inconsistent, and unable to operate continuously. There was a clear need for an automated, scalable monitoring solution.

## Solution

A deep learning-based computer vision pipeline that processes video feeds from fixed cameras and classifies detected vehicles as authorized or unauthorized based on zone rules and vehicle type.

### Pipeline

1. **Video ingestion**: Real-time streams from IP cameras installed at zone boundaries
2. **Object detection**: YOLO-based model detects and classifies vehicles (motorcycle, car, truck, bicycle)
3. **Zone compliance analysis**: Detected objects are mapped to geofenced pedestrian zones
4. **Alert generation**: Confirmed violations trigger timestamped alerts with cropped evidence images
5. **Dashboard**: Web interface for reviewing violations, statistics, and camera health

## Key Results

- High precision in motorcycle detection under variable lighting and partial occlusion
- Publicly released annotated dataset for motorcyclist detection in pedestrian areas
- Demonstrated feasibility for deployment at scale across multiple zones

A team of researchers from the Universidad Tecnológica de Bolívar developed an innovative approach to accurately calculate the Return on Investment (ROI) for photovoltaic (PV) systems. This method uniquely incorporates system degradation and maintenance factors, providing a more realistic ROI estimation.

About

Soy Doctor en Ingeniería por la Pontificia Universidad Javeriana (2023), Profesor Asociado y Director de Programas de Posgrado en la Universidad Tecnológica de Bolívar, e investigador principal del grupo VerbaNexAI. Cuento con amplia experiencia en IA, NLP, Big Data, Data Analytics e Ingeniería de Software, combinando práctica técnica con formación académica para entrenar a la próxima generación de profesionales tecnológicos.

Mis áreas de interés incluyen Inteligencia Artificial (Deep Learning, Transfer Learning, Reinforcement Learning), Procesamiento de Lenguaje Natural (perfilado de autores, análisis de sentimientos, recuperación de información), Visión por Computadora (detección de objetos) y análisis de señales multi-dominio. He participado activamente en competencias internacionales: IberLEF, SemEval, CLEF y PAN.

Soy IEEE Senior Member, ACM Senior Member y miembro fundador de la IEEE Colombian Caribbean Section. Best Paper Award en IEEE C3 2023.

Projects

Apollo

Oct 2023 – Present

Python package for multimodal AI understanding with three channels: Audio (Lyre), Text (Pythia) and Vision (Argus). Includes tools for head analysis, motor pattern diagnostics, facial movements and NLP with syllabification.

Multimodal AI Python Computer Vision NLP

TrueShield

Active

Fake news detection platform using entity recognition, cross-reference from RSS/Twitter/Reddit and a veracity scoring algorithm. Built on microservices with Angular, Django, FastAPI, Hugging Face models and deployed on AWS.

NLP Fake News FastAPI AWS

FrameLens

Active

NLP methodology for analyzing news framing in Spanish-language media. Identifies cultural and political factors shaping narratives; contributes to media literacy and cross-linguistic public opinion analysis.

NLP Media Framing Spanish

MentalGuard

Active

Multimodal AI system for early detection of mental health risk in university students. Integrates NLP techniques on text and audio to provide objective, rapid and scalable risk evaluation beyond traditional self-reporting.

Mental Health Multimodal Deep Learning

Mapping Motion

Active

Cognitive approach to analyzing movement patterns of individuals with dyspraxia using motion capture and advanced signal processing. Results have practical applications in rehabilitation and assistive technology design.

Signal Processing Computer Vision Healthcare

Traffic Monitoring

Jan 2023 – Present

Automated computer vision system for detecting unauthorized vehicle entries (motorcycles) in pedestrian zones in Cartagena. Developed for an energy company to improve urban pedestrian safety enforcement.

Computer Vision Object Detection Smart Cities

PV-ROI

2023

Innovative approach to calculate Return on Investment for photovoltaic systems incorporating system degradation and maintenance factors for a more realistic financial estimation over the asset lifecycle.

Photovoltaics Data Analytics Predictive Models
